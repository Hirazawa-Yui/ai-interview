# 02-接口清单

> 性质：参考文档 · 状态：基线 v1 · 更新日期：2026-09-05
> 约定：全部前缀 `/api`；普通接口返回 `Result<T>`（HTTP 恒 200，code 区分成败）；SSE 接口直接产 text/event-stream（无 Result 包装）。新增/变更接口必须同步本文档。

## 1. 文件分片上传（FileChunkController）

| 方法 | 路径 | 入参 | 出参(data) |
|---|---|---|---|
| POST | `/api/file/check` | {md5, fileName, fileSize, totalChunks} | {completed, url?, uploadId, totalChunks, uploadedChunks[]} |
| POST | `/api/file/chunk` | multipart: md5, chunkIndex(0-based), chunkMd5?, file | "分片 N 上传成功" |
| POST | `/api/file/merge` | {md5, fileName} | OSS url 字符串 |
| GET | `/api/file/preview/{md5}` | — | {completed, uploadId, totalChunks, uploadedChunks[]} |

## 2. 简历管理（ResumeController）

| 方法 | 路径 | 入参 | 出参(data) |
|---|---|---|---|
| POST | `/api/resumes/upload` | multipart: file（PDF/DOC/DOCX/TXT） | {resumeId, fileName, analyzeStatus, duplicate} |
| GET | `/api/resumes` | — | [{id, fileName, fileSize, analyzeStatus, latestScore, createdAt}] |
| GET | `/api/resumes/{id}/detail` | — | {id, fileName, ..., parsedText, analyzeStatus, analyzeError, analyses[]} |
| DELETE | `/api/resumes/{id}` | — | "删除成功" |
| POST | `/api/resumes/{id}/reanalyze` | — | "已触发重新分析" |

## 3. 知识库管理（KnowledgeBaseController）

| 方法 | 路径 | 入参 | 出参(data) |
|---|---|---|---|
| POST | `/api/knowledge/upload` | {fileKey, kbName, category} | {kbId, kbName, vectorStatus, duplicate} |
| GET | `/api/knowledge/list` | query: vectorStatus?, category?, sortBy(time/size/question) | KbListItemDTO[] |
| GET | `/api/knowledge/{id}` | — | {id, kbName, category, ..., parsedText, vectorStatus, chunkCount, questionCount} |
| DELETE | `/api/knowledge/{id}` | — | "删除成功" |
| PUT | `/api/knowledge/{id}/category` | {category} | "分类已更新" |
| POST | `/api/knowledge/{id}/revectorize` | — | "已触发重新向量化" |

## 4. RAG 问答（RagChatController）

| 方法 | 路径 | 类型 | 入参 | 出参 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| POST | `/api/knowledge/query/stream` | **SSE** | {knowledgeBaseIds[], question} | data: 文本片段流 |
| POST | `/api/rag-chat/sessions` | 普通 | {kbIds[], title?} | RagSessionDTO |
| GET | `/api/rag-chat/sessions` | 普通 | — | RagSessionDTO[]（isPinned DESC, updatedAt DESC） |
| GET | `/api/rag-chat/sessions/{id}` | 普通 | — | {id, sessionTitle, kbIds[], isPinned, createdAt, messages[]} |
| DELETE | `/api/rag-chat/sessions/{id}` | 普通 | — | "删除成功" |
| POST | `/api/rag-chat/sessions/{id}/messages/stream` | **SSE** | {question} | data: 文本片段流（完成后消息落库） |

## 5. 模拟面试（InterviewController）

| 方法 | 路径 | 入参 | 出参(data) |
|---|---|---|---|
| POST | `/api/interviews/sessions` | {resumeId?, jdText, direction, questionCount(5~15)} | {sessionId, direction, questionCount, firstQuestion} |
| GET | `/api/interviews/sessions` | — | [{id, direction, questionCount, currentQuestion, status, createdAt, updatedAt}] |
| GET | `/api/interviews/sessions/{id}` | — | {id, resumeId, jdText, direction, ..., questions[], answers[], status, evaluateError} |
| DELETE | `/api/interviews/sessions/{id}` | — | "删除成功"（级联答案+评估） |
| POST | `/api/interviews/sessions/{id}/answers` | {questionNumber, answerText} | {nextQuestion, progress} 或 {allDone, message, progress} |
| POST | `/api/interviews/sessions/{id}/evaluate` | — | {evaluateStatus:"EVALUATING", message} |
| GET | `/api/interviews/sessions/{id}/evaluation` | — | {sessionId, sessionStatus, evaluateStatus, overallScore?, summary?, perQuestion?, strengths?, improvements?, evaluatedAt?, error?}（轮询） |

## 6. 系统健康（HealthController）

| 方法 | 路径 | 出参(data) |
|---|---|---|
| GET | `/api/health` | {app:"UP", llm:{status,latency}, embedding:{status,latency,dimensions}, oss:{status,latency,bucket}} |

> ⚠️ /api/health 每次实时调 LLM chat + embedding + OSS listObjects，有 token 成本与延迟，勿高频轮询。
